1|Before you buy a walking pad, measure these three things.
1|Grab a tape measure first. It can save you a return.
2|One. Your floor lane. How many clear inches can you walk in a straight line?
3|Two. Your desk gap. Measure from the floor to the bottom of your desk frame.
4|Three. Your storage spot. Where will it live when it's folded? Behind a door, or under the bed?
5|Then compare your numbers to the deck size and the folded size on the product page.
6|While you measure, keep the lane clear of cords and rugs, and keep kids and pets out of the area.
7|Save this before you shop.
7|Where in your place would it go?
